Ring experiment of the sm100 Gluon kernel, one B200, 2026-10-23. The experiment code (not kept)
gave the row tiles and the weight tiles rings of their own, each with its own copy warp. With
early release, the dequantization warps free a weight stage after they read it. Without it, the
completion of the multiply frees it. Ring = (row stages, weight stages, early release).
(3, 3, False) behaves like the kernel before (one ring of 3 stages).
